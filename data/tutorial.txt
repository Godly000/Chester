Check your resources and other currencies with "/profile currency".
Check your stats with "/profile statistics".
Obstacles randomly spawn around your village. "/check_obstacles" saves them to your village, "/profile obstacles" shows how many of each type there are, and "/remove_obstacle" removes them for a small amount of Gems.
Ran out of space to store loot? Either upgrade your storages or Clan Castle (Resources category).
Magic items are quite valuable. "/profile Magic Item" shows you what Magic Items you have, "/sell" shows how much they can be sold for, and "/use [Magic Item name]" uses one of your choice.
Want to see how your Buliders and Researchers are doing? "/profile upgrade" shows what all your Upgraders are working on and when they will finish.
How upgraded is your village? Use "/profile [Category name] Level" to find out!
Which Cosmetics and Hero Equipments you own, and which are you still looking for? Check it with "/profile [Category name]".
Nowhere to spend your hard-earned resources? Upgrade some buildings using the "/upgrade [Category name] [Item name]" command.
Want to upgrade your Town Hall? Use the "/remaining" command to check which upgrades are left.
Started the wrong upgrade? Use the "/cancel" command to get half of your resources back.
Don't leave your Collectors, Mines, and Drills full! "/collect_loot" will generate passive resources for you.
Need resources in a pinch? Transfer some of your Treasury resources to your storages with "/collect_treasury".
Use the "/view_collectors" command to see how well your Resource generators are doing without collecting right away.
Type "/help" to see a list of all the commands.
Do you enjoy playing Chester? Donnations are accepted through "/support"!
